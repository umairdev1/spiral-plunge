Spiral Plunge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Spiral Plunge ("the app") is a game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not need an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as level, coins, unlocked balls, sound and vibration settings) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes this data.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission only, to give haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
